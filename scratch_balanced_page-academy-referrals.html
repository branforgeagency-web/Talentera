1
Share academy link
Pick any academy card. Click "📤 Refer Friend" → get unique tracking link. Share via WhatsApp / any channel.
2
Friend enrolls
Friend clicks link → sees academy details → enrolls. Talentera notifies academy: "Referred by Ananya."

After friend completes 30 days (retention check), academy pays you. Option A: direct UPI. Option B: via Talentera in 7 days.
